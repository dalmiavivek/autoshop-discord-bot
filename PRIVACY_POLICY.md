# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Application:** Vampire Martt Auto Shop Discord Bot  

---

## 1. Information We Collect
The Vampire Martt Discord Bot only collects and processes the minimum necessary information required to facilitate shop commands and automated role assignments:
- **Discord User ID & Username**: To identify your account, grant server roles upon purchase, and verify admin permissions.
- **Order / Invoice IDs**: Stored locally in our database upon redemption via `/verify` to ensure orders cannot be claimed more than once.
- **Discord Server ID**: To register slash commands and send restock announcements to your configured channels.

We do NOT collect, store, or have access to your personal financial details, credit card numbers, or passwords. All payment transactions are processed externally through official payment gateways on Shoppex.io.

---

## 2. How Information is Used
Data collected is strictly used to:
- Deliver purchased digital goods and verify completed orders.
- Grant the Discord Customer Role to buyers.
- Prevent fraudulent or duplicate order redemptions.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Security
- Verified order records are stored in a secure local database.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to any third parties.

---

## 4. Contact
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data removal, please contact the server administration via Discord support.
